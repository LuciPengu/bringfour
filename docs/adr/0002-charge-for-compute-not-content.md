# Monetization: charge for compute and tooling, never Pokémon content

The Copilot bills in Credits redeemed for Reports (Fable API runs), with free Credits for new users. Payment is framed everywhere as buying analysis compute — never Pokémon data, sprites, or content — and every surface carries the "unofficial, not affiliated with Nintendo/Creatures Inc./GAME FREAK/The Pokémon Company" disclaimer.

Why this shape: research (Aug 2026) found The Pokémon Company has never acted against a stats/teambuilding tool — including monetized ones (Pikalytics' paid app, LimitlessTCG's Patreon, Poké Genie's subscriptions) — while its former counsel states monetization is what draws enforcement *attention*, with actual action reserved for fan games and product substitutes. The community-standard posture (pay-for-tooling framing + disclaimer + no game-like features) has a 100% survival rate; flat "unlimited AI" subscriptions were separately rejected because unbounded Fable usage (~$10/$50 per MTok) makes per-user cost uncappable.

## Consequences

- Report cost must be metered per run (task budgets / max_tokens caps) so a Credit has a known COGS.
- The product must never grow features that play like a Pokémon game or substitute for one.
